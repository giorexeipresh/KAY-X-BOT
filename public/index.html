How it works

  1. Choose pairing code or QR above.
  2. In WhatsApp open Settings → Linked devices → Link a device.
  3. Enter the code, or scan the QR from another device.
  4. Send .menu in any chat. Shadows, arise!

To unlink: WhatsApp → Linked devices → tap the device → Log out. The bot then deletes your login from this server.
